# Site Brain 7.2.12

Includes the 7.2.11 30-second retry and stalled-learning fixes.

## Explain the lesson and let the user demonstrate
The Learning screen now shows the exact bound action and typed pass conditions before it runs, the latest verification outcome, and whether the full lesson verified. Alternative checks are shown as OR, independent requirements as AND. This explanation is task-local UI state, never diagnostics or long-term memory. The browser offers the same details with a tap. “Pause and teach this step” opens the existing live session without navigating away; teaching help explains how to demonstrate and finish the existing teaching flow. A verified step is explicitly distinguished from a verified full procedure.

## Repair demonstration learning
Capture the initial page before recording, serialize recorder start/events/completion, and exclude auth, unknown, commit and off-site actions. Typed traces now become parameterized training proposals with capability tags and zero success credit. The existing strict TRAIN executor can test a same-site proposal when its control and lesson match. Only end-to-end verifier success promotes it to a verified reusable procedure and curriculum completion. Failed proposals remain on hold. This does not retroactively promote old unverified demonstrations.

Finishing a demonstration launches a focused TRAIN check directly, without the overnight probe that could skip its initial HOME page. Pending same-site proposals take priority during training when their exact entry controls match; ordinary searches cannot use them until verified.

Focused checks support safe filter openers, filter application, and category openers as prerequisite procedures, without crediting unrelated curriculum lessons.
