# AI Browser 7.2.10

The two October 10 exports are identical: 6,376 events, including 3,752 from 7.2.9. The new-build portion contains 348 lesson outcomes (2 DONE), 806 action outcomes and 534 opportunity rechecks. Facebook has 411 no-target-on-filter-panel rechecks, 47 failed search groundings despite visible search controls; OfferUp has 36 failed dialog-close groundings despite visible closers. KSL Classifieds has 139 partial pagination lessons, usually after three discovery scrolls. Unrestricted AI calls, including the smarter model, are present; increasing request allowance alone is not resolving these failures.

## Engine changes

- Old role-wide failures remain stored and influence ranking, but their penalty cannot hide a confidently classified exact live control. Weak and fallback controls keep the stricter penalty. Failed procedure cooldowns, per-task repeat limits, execution interlocks and verification remain required.
- Footer placement no longer penalizes next/previous/load-more pagination controls as footer noise.
- Pagination opportunity signatures use only the corresponding pagination controls. Reaching the footer, changing scroll position or exposing unrelated filters does not clear the lesson cooldown. A newly exposed real pagination control still reopens it. Unavailable controls never receive success credit.
- A settled, non-dialog filter panel with no unfinished lesson targets can return to the existing allowed search URL when a lesson is due. Live lesson controls, dialogs, auth/challenge pages and cooldowns prevent that recovery.
- Diagnostics identify seek_pagination directly and include scroll dimensions and next/load-more control counts. They include no content, queries or credentials.

The two persistent unrestricted AI choices and saved learning remain intact. These fixes address reproduced engine causes; live phone progress still needs the next diagnostics, and a site without a capability may retain an unverified lesson.

## Validation

Four regression tests reproduce failures before the production changes. Tests also verify weak/fallback controls stay rejected, real next controls reopen cooldowns without completing lessons, and recovery navigation creates no success credit. Full core, browser, Android and stable-signing checks gate publication.
